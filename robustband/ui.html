    From two sets of results to a clear comparison

    What does robustband do?

    It checks a comparison across scoring rules you explicitly choose. A result can stay the same across every rule, or change when you choose another rule.

    What do I need?

    Two sets of outputs or measurements on the same samples, in one paired file. Start with the template for your data kind.

    How do I use it?

    1. Run the built-in example to see the result.
    2. Download a template, replace the example rows and choose your file.
    3. Confirm the rules, run the comparison and save the report.

    What does a stable result mean?

    Every selected rule supports the declared preference on the supplied records. Choose rules that fit your task; the count of supporting rules is not a confidence level.

    Use your own data in the local app

    This page lets you explore a saved example without installing anything. The local app calculates results for your own files.

    1. Extract the full edition folder.
    2. Windows portable needs no Python installation; the source edition needs Python 3.9+. Double-click start_robustband.cmd in that folder.
    3. In the browser page that opens, choose your data kind and select your file.
